- PLP-385: `registry rebind <tracker> <old-repository> <new-repository> [--marker-only]` relinks a transferred or renamed repository to its existing tracker project: it reads the project back from the provider with the new identity, then rewrites the entry (extra fields kept) and the marker (activation and migration digest kept), refusing with the steps to follow when the provider does not carry the new identity; `--marker-only` republishes another checkout's marker without writing the registry. Documented in `README.md`, `docs/tracker-contract.md` and `skills/doctor/SKILL.md`.
